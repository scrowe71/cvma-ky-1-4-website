# Editing the website (no code needed)

Officers edit the site at **https://app.pagescms.org**.

## First time
1. Open the invitation email from Pages CMS and follow the link, or ask the Webmaster for an invite.
2. Sign in, open **cvma-ky-1-4-website**, and make sure the branch shown at the top is **dev**.

## Making a change
1. Pick a section on the left: Home page, About page, Officers, Calendar events, Photo albums or Contact page.
2. Edit the form. Lists (officers, events, albums, photos) have **Add** and remove buttons, and items can be dragged to reorder.
3. Click **Save**.
4. About a minute later, check the change at **https://dev.cvma-ky-1-4-website.pages.dev**.
5. Tell the Webmaster it's ready. The Webmaster reviews it and publishes it to the public site.

## Tips
- **Times:** use the 24-hour clock, e.g. `09:00`, `13:00`, `17:30`.
- **Addresses:** a full street address adds a Google Maps link automatically.
- **Linking photos to an event:** type the photo album's title exactly in the event's "Photo album" box.
- **Photos:** please use reasonably sized pictures (phone photos are fine), avoid readable license plates, and only post people who are OK being on a public website.
- **Text formatting** (About and Contact): bold, italic, bullet lists, headings and links are supported.
- **Mistakes** can always be undone: every save is kept in the site's history.
